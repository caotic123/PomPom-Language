\textbf{Computation and conversion.}
Write $\longmapsto$ for a root computation, $\leadsto$ for its compatible
closure together with $\eta$ contraction, and $\to$ for the operational
relation in Rocq.
\[
\begin{aligned}
 (\lambda x.\,b)\,a&\longmapsto b[a/x],&
 \op{fst}(a,b)&\longmapsto a,&
 \op{snd}(a,b)&\longmapsto b,\\
 \lambda x.\,f\,x&\leadsto f\quad(x\notin\op{FV}(f)),&
 (\op{fst}\,p,\op{snd}\,p)&\leadsto p.
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
\inferrule{t\longmapsto u}{t\leadsto u}
\qquad
\inferrule{t\leadsto u}{\mathcal C[t]\leadsto\mathcal C[u]}
\qquad
\inferrule{}{t\equiv t}
\qquad
\inferrule{t\leadsto u}{t\equiv u}
\qquad
\inferrule{t\equiv u}{u\equiv t}
\qquad
\inferrule{t\equiv u\\u\equiv v}{t\equiv v}
\]
Here $\mathcal C[-]$ ranges over single-hole term contexts, including
binders. There is no equation unfolding a $\op{close}$ type.
The remaining root computations appear beside their operators below.

\textbf{Induction motive and method, expanded.}
\[

 &\hspace{8mm}\allh\,(H\,j)\,X_G\,y_s\,P^G\to P\,H\,j\,(\roll\,y_s).

\ruleof{ty_close_ind}{\jd{I}{\Set_0}\\\jd{G}{\Defs\,I}\\

 \op{closeInd}\,G\,P\,s\,F\,i\,(\roll\,x_s)
