\sectiontitle{13}{Selected theorem statements and proof status}
Of the 47 original claims, 46 have proofs without axiom dependencies and
full preservation is refuted. No conjectures remain. Progress, operational
preservation, confluence, structural rules, elaboration and subtyping
soundness, full beta/eta strong normalization, consistency, coercion and
checking coherence, and their corollaries are proved. Both coherence
theorems are proved through a binary relational model on closed terms
(\texttt{progress/OpenSignaturesRel*.v}); coercions are compared through
semantic coercion graphs that retag rows by constructor name.
The current rules admit the following checked counterexample:
\[
 f=(\lambda z.\lambda B.z)(\lambda x.x),\qquad
 T=\Pi A:\Set_0.\,A\to A,\qquad
 \vdash\lambda A.f\,A:T,\quad \lambda A.f\,A\leadsto f,\quad
 \nvdash f:T.
\]
The hidden type of $z$ may depend on $A$ inside the eta expansion, but must
be fixed before $B$ is bound in $f$.
\texttt{OpenSignaturesEtaPolymorphism.v} proves this without axioms.
The false conjecture was removed; its exact proposition remains as
\texttt{full\_preservation\_statement}. A specification repair is required.
The exact dependency table is in \texttt{progress/PROOF\_STATUS.md}.
\[
\begin{array}{@{}ll@{}}
 \text{Context validity}&
 \Gamma\vdash t:A\ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma\ \op{wf}.\\[4pt]
 \text{Type correctness}&
 \Gamma\vdash t:A\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists k.\ \Gamma\vdash A:\Set_k.\\[4pt]
 \text{Weakening}&
 \Gamma\vdash t:A,\ \Gamma\vdash B:\Set_k
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma,x:B\vdash t:A.\\[4pt]
 \text{Substitution}&
 \Gamma,x:A\vdash t:B,\ \Gamma\vdash u:A
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma\vdash t[u/x]:B[u/x].\\[4pt]
 \text{Preservation}&
 \Gamma\vdash t:A,\ t\to u
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma\vdash u:A
 \quad\text{(full }\leadsto\text{ is refuted).}\\[4pt]
 \text{Progress}&
 \varnothing\vdash t:A
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \op{value}(t)\ \lor\ \exists u.\ t\to u.\\[4pt]
 \text{Normalization}&
 \Gamma\vdash t:A\ \Longrightarrow\ t\text{ is strongly normalizing for }\leadsto.\\[4pt]
 \text{Confluence}&
 \Gamma\vdash t:A,\ t\leadsto^*u,\ t\leadsto^*v\\
 &\hspace{6mm}\Longrightarrow\ \exists w,w'.\
   u\leadsto^*w\land v\leadsto^*w'\land w\equiv_\alpha w'.\\[4pt]
 \text{Consistency}&
 \neg\exists t.\ \varnothing\vdash t:\bot.\\[4pt]
 \text{Dead soundness}&
 \dead{D}{X}{d}\ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma\vdash d:\interp{D}{X}\to\bot.\\[4pt]
 \text{Description soundness}&
 \dsub{D}{D'}{X}{q}\ \Longrightarrow\

 \text{Coercion soundness}&
 \subt{A}{B}{c}\ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma\vdash c:A\to B.\\[4pt]
 \text{Synthesis soundness}&
 \synth{e}{A}{t}\ \Longrightarrow\ \Gamma\vdash t:A.\\[4pt]
 \text{Checking soundness}&

 \text{Coercion coherence}&

 \text{Roll/unroll}&
 \Gamma\vdash x:C_{F,G}(i)
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \roll(\unroll_{F,G,i}\,x)\approx_{C_{F,G}(i)}x.
\end{array}
\]
The last statement additionally requires
$\Gamma\vdash I:\Set_0$, $F,G:\Defs\,I$, and $i:I$.
Weakening uses a fresh $x$.
$\approx_A$ quantifies over typed closing substitutions and closed Boolean
observations. It is not definitional equality.
