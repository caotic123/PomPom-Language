13.     Selected theorem statements and proof status
Of the 47 original claims in OpenSignaturesTheorems.v, 27 have proofs without axiom dependencies, 16 have
conditional proofs, and four remain conjectures. Progress, confluence, weakening, substitution, type correctness,
closing substitution, and close canonical forms are proved. Full preservation, strong normalization, and the two
coherence claims remain unproved. The function-cumulativity repair addresses the previous eta counterexample; its
regression proofs do not prove general preservation. The exact dependency table is in progress/PROOF_STATUS.md.

                  Context validity              Γ ⊢ t : A =⇒ Γ wf.
                  Type correctness              Γ ⊢ t : A =⇒ ∃k. Γ ⊢ A : Setk .
                  Weakening                     Γ ⊢ t : A, Γ ⊢ B : Setk =⇒ Γ, x : B ⊢ t : A.
                  Substitution                  Γ, x : A ⊢ t : B, Γ ⊢ u : A =⇒ Γ ⊢ t[u/x] : B[u/x].
                  Preservation                  Γ ⊢ t : A, t → u =⇒ Γ ⊢ u : A      (also for ⇝ ).
                  Progress                      ∅ ⊢ t : A =⇒ value(t) ∨ ∃u. t → u.
                  Normalization                 Γ ⊢ t : A =⇒ t is strongly normalizing for ⇝ .
                  Confluence                    Γ ⊢ t : A, t ⇝∗ u, t ⇝∗ v
                                                    =⇒ ∃w, w′ . u ⇝∗ w ∧ v ⇝∗ w′ ∧ w ≡α w′ .
                  Consistency                   ¬∃t. ∅ ⊢ t : ⊥.
                  Dead soundness                Γ ⊢ D deadIX ⇝ d =⇒ Γ ⊢ d : JDK X → ⊥.
                  Description soundness         Γ ⊢ D ⪯IX D′ ⇝ q =⇒ Γ ⊢ q : JDK X → JD′ K X.
                  Coercion soundness            Γ ⊢ A ⊑ B ⇝ c =⇒ Γ ⊢ c : A → B.
                  Synthesis soundness           Γ ⊢ e ⇒ A ⇝ t =⇒ Γ ⊢ t : A.
                  Checking soundness            Γ ⊢ e ⇐ A ⇝ t =⇒ Γ ⊢ t : A.
                  Coercion coherence            Γ ⊢ A ⊑ B ⇝ c, Γ ⊢ A ⊑ B ⇝ d =⇒ c ≈A→B d.
                  Checking coherence            Γ ⊢ e ⇐ A ⇝ t, Γ ⊢ e ⇐ A ⇝ u =⇒ t ≈A u.
